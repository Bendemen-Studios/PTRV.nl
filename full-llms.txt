## Business summary
Petrov's Workshop offers 3D-printing from Hellevoetsluis in the Netherlands. Customers must supply their own print-ready 3D model. Confirm printability, material options, pricing and planning directly with the workshop.

Potential project types include:
- Functional accessories.
- Custom parts and practical components.
- Prototypes and unique objects.
- Accessories for desks, gaming setups and cable management.
- Other suitable objects, depending on dimensions, geometry, material requirements and print feasibility.

These examples describe potential project types, not a guarantee that every design can be produced.

## Scope and limitations
- Customers must provide their own completed, print-ready 3D file.
- STL and 3MF are the model formats named on the website.
- Petrov's Workshop does not create 3D designs, design objects from scratch, or model new 3D files.
- A submitted file may need review before the workshop can confirm feasibility.
- Not every size, shape, color, material, tolerance or mechanical property will be possible.
- Do not represent the workshop as offering CAD design or 3D modelling services.

## How the process works
1. The customer contacts the workshop and provides a print-ready file, preferably STL or 3MF.
2. The customer describes the intended use, dimensions and relevant preferences.
3. The workshop reviews printability and discusses material, pricing and expected timing.
4. After the customer approves the agreed price, payment is made in advance.
5. Printing proceeds after payment is received.
6. Delivery or collection arrangements are discussed directly with the customer.

The actual feasibility, price and lead time depend on the project and should be confirmed directly.
